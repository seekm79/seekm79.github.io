Dialbook is a CRM for people who work from a list of people to call: sales follow-up, debt collection, or both. Import a spreadsheet, dial through it, record what happened, and never miss a callback or a promise to pay.

Sales

Call prospects and move them on.

Leads move through New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation and Won or Lost. Calls move them on their own, callbacks come first in the queue, and the win rate is always one tap away.

Collections

Chase overdue invoices without losing track.

Record promises to pay with an amount and a date. When the day passes without payment the account joins the broken-promise queue and comes first in the dialer. Aging buckets show where the money is.

— Features

Everything a caller needs,
nothing a caller doesn't.

☎

A dialer that orders the day

Callbacks due and broken promises come first, then the most valuable. Tap the number, talk, come back, pick the result, save and next.

⇪

Import Excel or CSV

Match the columns and go. Big sheets, even a million rows, import in the background while you keep working. Duplicates are left out.

✓

Promises, payments, aging

Promise to pay, partial payment, paid in full. Accounts grouped by how late they are, with a customer statement as a PDF on request.

✉

WhatsApp, SMS and email templates

Templates with fill-ins for name, amount and due date, in several languages, with pictures and PDFs attached. WhatsApp or your email app opens with the message ready.

▦

Tasks and alerts

Callbacks, appointments, follow-ups and things you are waiting for. Home shows what is late or due today, with a map of the day's visits.

◔

22 reports

Sales performance, payments, target pace, collections forecast, recovery by age, deal flow and what works. Share as PDF or CSV, or print.

✦

Bring your own AI

Connect OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek and others. Off by default, with a data notice and masking that strips names and numbers before sending.

⚿

Encrypted backups

One file, encrypted with your password (AES-256). Save it to Google Drive, email or your computer and restore it on a new phone.

▣

Share a lead by QR code

Hand a lead to a teammate by showing a QR code. Nothing goes through a server or the internet; the lead travels inside the code.

— What your customer receives

A message that reads
like you wrote it.

Pick a template on the contact, and Dialbook fills in the name, company, amount and due date, adds the brochure or price list you attached, and opens WhatsApp with everything ready. The customer sees a normal message from your number. Nothing passes through Dialbook's servers, because there are none.

  • Fill-ins: name, company, amount, due date, your name and company.
  • Pictures and PDFs, up to five, sent with the message.
  • A version per language: customers with Malay or Chinese set get that one first.
  • Every send is recorded on the contact under Messages sent.

— Privacy

Your customers are
your business.

Read the privacy policy →

Stored on the phone only

Contacts, notes, amounts and passwords never reach us. There is no account and no server, and no advertising or analytics in the app.

Two ways data leaves, both yours

When you create a backup file, and when you turn on your own AI provider. Both are off until you act.

Masking before AI

Turn on masking and names, companies, emails and phone numbers are removed before anything is sent to your provider.

Erase everything in one tap

Settings › Privacy and data removes every list, note and setting from the phone.

Support

Stuck on something?

The help centre covers every screen with pictures, from first setup to restoring a backup on a new phone. For anything else, write to us and we reply within two working days.
