Getting started

Getting started

Dialbook keeps your call lists, calls, promises and payments on this phone. There is no account and no server.

First time

  1. Choose what you use your phone calls for: Sales, Collections or both.
  2. Leave Start with sample contacts on to try the app with a few sample contacts, or turn it off to start empty.
  3. Tap Start.

Then

  • Bring in your customers from Excel or CSV: Import an Excel or CSV sheet.
  • Open a campaign and tap Start dialing to work through it: Work through a campaign.

Sales, collections or both

The app shows only the kinds of work you choose. Someone who only collects never sees a sales tab.

To change it

  1. Go to More › What I use the app for.
  2. Turn Sales or Collections on or off.

Turning one off hides its tab, reports and alerts. Its campaigns and contacts stay on the phone and come back when you turn it on again.

Import an Excel or CSV sheet

  1. Go to More › Import Excel or CSV (or the same row in Sales / Collect).
  2. Tap Choose Excel or CSV file and pick an .xlsx or .csv file.
  3. Match the columns: at least a name and a phone number. Company, email, amount, due date and language are optional.
  4. Choose what These contacts are for, give the list a name (List name), check the preview and import.

Good to know

  • Big sheets (even a million rows) import in the background; a bar at the bottom shows progress and you can keep working. Cancel removes everything it added.
  • Repeated phone numbers in the file are left out.
  • Numbers already marked Do not call or Invalid number arrive marked, and the summary says how many.
  • Each import becomes a new campaign.

Add a contact by hand

  1. Open Sales or Collect.
  2. Tap + at the top, or Add lead / Add account under the campaigns.
  3. Enter at least a name and a phone number, choose the campaign, and save.

Good to know

  • A number already in that campaign is refused, so you don't call the same person twice.
  • A number marked Do not call or Invalid number in another campaign arrives already marked: The blocked-numbers list.
  • Many contacts at once? Import a sheet instead: Import an Excel or CSV sheet.

Find a contact

  1. On Home, tap the round magnifier at the top right.
  2. Type part of a name, company, email or phone number. Phone numbers match however they are written ("012-338 4471" or "0123384471").

Inside a campaign, the search box at the top of its list searches only that campaign.

Edit a contact

  1. Open the contact.
  2. Tap the pencil (Edit) at the top right.
  3. Change the name, numbers, email, company or amounts, and save.

Changing a phone number clears its mark (Invalid number, Do not call), unless the new number is on the blocked list.

Using an iPad or tablet

  • The menu sits on the left. The arrows at its top widen it to show labels, or shrink it to icons for more room.
  • Lists open their items beside them: tap a campaign or customer and it shows on the right.
  • Turn the tablet sideways for two columns on Home, the contact page and the dialer.

Language

Go to More › Language. Same as the phone follows your phone. Dates, numbers and the AI's answers follow the language.

Currency

Go to More › Currency. It changes how amounts are shown; amounts are not converted.

Dark mode

Go to More › Appearance and choose Same as the phone, Light or Dark.

Calling

Work through a campaign

  1. Open Sales or Collect and tap a campaign.
  2. Tap the green Start dialing button.
  3. Tap the big phone number: your phone's dialer opens. Talk.
  4. Come back, pick the Result of this call, add a note if needed, and tap Save and next.

The order puts callbacks due and broken promises first, then the most valuable. Skip moves on without logging. Numbers marked Do not call or Invalid number are never dialled.

Need a moment with this customer first (a task, a WhatsApp)? Tap Save instead: the call is logged and they stay on screen until you tap Next.

Log what happened on a call

After the call, pick one result under Result of this call:

  • Callback: choose the day; a call-back task is made: Call backs.
  • Promise to pay: enter the amount and the date they promised: Record a promise to pay.
  • Partial payment / Paid in full: records the payment: Record a payment.
  • Wrong number: marks that number Invalid number everywhere.
  • Your own results: Your own call results.

Add what was said in the note box, then Save and next. The result, note and time go into the contact's Call history.

Skip a contact

In the dialer, tap Skip to move to the next contact without logging anything. They stay in the queue and come up again next time.

Call backs

Logging Callback sets the day to call again. On that day the contact comes first in the dialer, shows in Up next on Home, and appears as a Call back task.

  • Change the day or add a time in the task: open All tasks and tap it.
  • Ticking the task off clears the call back.
  • Any other result on the next call clears it too.

Your own call results

  1. Go to More › Call outcomes.
  2. Tap Add your own, type a name (e.g. "Sent quotation"), and choose Counts as, so reports count it right.

Renaming a result or changing what it counts as updates past calls too.

Mark a number: OK, invalid or do not call

  1. Open the contact and tap the phone number.
  2. Choose OK, Invalid number or Do not call.

In the dialer, Mark this number under the call button does the same.

  • Marked numbers are never dialled; with a second number left, that one is used.
  • A contact with no number left to call drops out of the dialer and "left to call".
  • Do not call still lets you WhatsApp or email, with a warning first.
  • The mark applies to that number in every campaign: The blocked-numbers list.

The blocked-numbers list

Every number marked Invalid number or Do not call goes on one list for the whole app. Numbers match by their last 9 digits, so "012-338 4471" and "+60 12-338 4471" are the same number.

  • Marking a number in one campaign marks it in all of them.
  • Imports, new contacts and shared leads arrive already marked; the import summary says how many.
  • Setting it back to OK clears it everywhere.

To mark a number

  1. Open the contact and tap the phone number.
  2. Choose Do not call or Invalid number (or OK to clear it).

More: Mark a number: OK, invalid or do not call.

Call length from the phone's call log (Android)

On Android the app can read the length of each call from the phone's call log and attach it to the logged call.

  1. Go to More › Phone call log.
  2. Turn on Attach call time and length automatically and allow the permission.

iPhones don't let apps read the call log, so this is Android only.

Sales

Pipeline stages

Sales leads move through New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, then Won or Lost.

  • Calls move them on their own: a first answered call makes a New lead Contacted; Interested makes it Qualified.
  • To set the stage yourself, open the contact and tap Stage.
  • See all stages in Sales › Pipeline, and how long deals stay in each in the Deal flow report: Deal flow report.

Mark a deal won or lost

  • After a call, pick Won or Not interested.
  • Or open the contact, tap Stage and choose Won or Lost.

Won and lost deals are closed: they leave the dialer queue and count in the win rate and the Sales performance report: Sales performance report.

Say what a campaign is about

A short line like "Office cleaning, 10% off for new customers" helps you on calls and helps the AI write messages and scripts for that campaign.

  1. Open the campaign.
  2. Tap What this campaign is about and type it.

Share a lead with a teammate

  1. Open the contact.
  2. Tap the QR icon (Share lead) at the top.
  3. Let your teammate scan the QR code with their phone. Include my last note adds your last call note.

Your teammate scans it in Receive a lead: Receive a lead from a teammate. Nothing goes through a server or the internet; the lead travels inside the QR code.

Receive a lead from a teammate

  1. Go to More › Receive a lead (also in Sales / Collect).
  2. The camera opens. Point it at the QR code on your teammate's phone (they open the contact and tap Share lead).
  3. Check the lead, choose Add to campaign, and tap Add lead.

If the number is already in your list, you are told before adding. The added lead gets a call back for today with your teammate's note.

Collections

Record a promise to pay

  1. After the call, pick Promise to pay.
  2. Check Amount promised and Promised payment date, then save.

The promise shows on the contact under Promises to pay, in Collect › Promises, and on Home on the day it is due. If the day passes without payment, it becomes broken: Broken promises.

Record a payment

  • During a call: pick Partial payment (enter Amount paid) or Paid in full.
  • Any time: open the contact and tap Record payment.

The amount owed goes down, a promise it meets is marked kept, and the payment shows under Payments received and in the reports.

Broken promises

A promise is broken when its day passes without payment. Those accounts come first in the dialer, show in Needs attention on Home, and are marked on their row.

To chase them: tap the broken-promise line in Needs attention, then work down the list, or open the Collections forecast to see which promises are likely to come in: Collections forecast report.

Money owed by how late it is

Accounts are grouped by how late payment is: not yet due, 1–30 days, 31–60 days and over 60 days.

  • Home shows the totals; tap a bar to see who is in it.
  • Collect › Accounts lists accounts by how late they are.
  • The Recovery by age report shows how much of each group came in, so you can chase where it pays: Recovery by age report.

A customer statement

  1. Open the contact.
  2. Tap Customer statement (Calls, promises and payments as a PDF to share).
  3. Share the PDF by WhatsApp or email.

Tasks and alerts

Tasks

Tasks are things to do: Call back, Appointment, WhatsApp, Email, Research, Waiting for info or Other.

  • See them all in Tasks: Overdue, Due today and Coming up, or Done. Open it from Home › All tasks.
  • Tap the circle, or swipe the task to the right, to tick it off. Swipe it to the left to move it to Tomorrow.
  • Tap a task to open it on one page: Call now, Send WhatsApp or Send email, change the day with one tap, add Updates, and tap Mark as done. Every change saves at once.
  • Sending a WhatsApp or email to that contact ticks off a matching open task.

Add one: Add a task.

Add a task

  • From a contact: in their Tasks group, tap Add task.
  • From the dialer: tap Add task below the note.
  • On its own: in Tasks, tap +.

Choose the Kind, write what to do (or what you are waiting for), pick the day (Today, Tomorrow or Pick a day), add a time if you like, and tap Save.

Needs attention

The Needs attention box on Home shows what is late or due today: overdue tasks, tasks due today, promises to pay due today and accounts with a broken promise. Each line opens the list behind it.

The red number on Home counts what is due today or late. When nothing is, it disappears.

Monthly targets

  1. Go to More › Monthly targets.
  2. Enter Deal value to win each month and/or Money to collect each month.

The Target pace report then shows how far you are, how much a day you still need, and where this pace ends the month: Target pace report.

Messages

Send a WhatsApp from a template

  1. Open the contact (or use WhatsApp in the dialer).
  2. Tap WhatsApp, pick a template, check the text and send.

WhatsApp opens with the message typed in; you press send there. The send is recorded on the contact under WhatsApp and email sent.

Make or change templates: Make or change message templates.

Send an email from a template

  1. Open the contact and tap Email.
  2. Pick a template, check the subject and text, and choose your email app.

Your email app opens with it filled in; you press send there. The send is recorded under WhatsApp and email sent.

Make or change message templates

  1. Go to More › Message templates.
  2. Choose WhatsApp or Email, then tap + for a new one, or a template to change it.
  3. Give it a name and the text. Tap the name tags to insert placeholders: Placeholders in messages.
  • Create with AI writes a draft from a short description (with an AI provider set up).
  • Make a version in another language makes a copy in another language; customers with that language set get it first.

Placeholders in messages

Placeholders are filled in for each contact when you send:

  • Name: the contact's name
  • Company: their company
  • Amount: deal value or amount owed
  • Due date: the due date
  • My name / My company: you and your company (set under AI assistant › About you)

The editor warns about a placeholder it doesn't know.

Your name and what you sell

Go to More › AI assistant › About you and fill in Your name, Company and What I sell. Messages use them in My name and My company, and the AI uses what you sell for scripts.

AI assistant

Set up the AI assistant

  1. Go to More › AI assistant.
  2. Choose a Provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek and others, or a model on your own computer), enter the API key, stored on this phone only and choose a model.
  3. Read the data notice and tap Turn on.

The key is stored on this phone only. Nothing is sent until you turn it on. Privacy: What the AI sees.

What the AI sees

The assistant looks up what it needs on this phone; only those answers go to your AI provider, never your whole database.

Turn on Remove names and phone numbers before sending (in AI assistant) and names, companies, emails and phone numbers are taken out before anything is sent. Contacts are then known to the AI by number only, and the app shows the real names in the chat.

Show or hide the AI

Go to More › AI assistant and turn Show AI in the app on or off. Off hides Ask AI about your work on Home and the AI buttons on contacts; your settings and chats are kept.

AI chats and history

  • New chat (the pencil at the top right) starts a new chat; the current one is kept.
  • Delete chat (the red bin at the top) deletes the chat on screen, after asking.
  • History (the clock) lists earlier chats: tap one to read or continue it, swipe left to delete it.
  • Each question sends only the latest part of a chat, and a chat holds 20 questions, to keep each question cheap. For a new topic, start a new chat.
  • "Looked up: …" above an answer says which of your data it used. An answer marked "not checked" gave figures without looking them up: ask again.

Let AI write a template

  1. In Message templates, tap Create with AI.
  2. Describe it under What is it for? (e.g. "polite reminder, invoice 30 days late"), choose the language, and tap Write draft.
  3. Check the draft and save it.

Only your description and what you sell go to your AI provider, never customer details. Needs an AI provider: Set up the AI assistant.

Reports

Reports

Tap Reports and choose one. Each shows the report as a printed page, with a chart where it helps.

  • Summaries by day, week or month: Sales performance report, Payments performance report, Touches report.
  • Where to act: Target pace report, Collections forecast report, Recovery by age report, Deal flow report, What works report.
  • Change the period and grouping: Change what a report covers. Share or print: Share or print a report.

Change what a report covers

At the top of a report:

  • Period: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time.
  • Group by: Day, Week or Month, for the summary reports.
  • Covers: everything, sales only or collections only, when you do both.

The figures, chart and table all follow. A report opened from an AI answer opens set the way the answer used it.

Share or print a report

  1. Open the report.
  2. Tap the share button at the top right.
  3. Choose Share as PDF, Share as CSV (Excel) or Print.

The CSV holds every row, even when the screen shows the first part of a long report; it opens in Excel.

Backup and data

Make a backup

  1. Go to More › Backup and restore.
  2. Set a Backup password (keep it safe: without it the backup cannot be opened).
  3. Tap Create backup and share and save the file to Google Drive, email or your computer.

The file is encrypted. Remind me to back up sets how often Home reminds you.

Restore a backup

  1. Go to More › Backup and restore.
  2. Tap Restore from a backup file, pick the file and enter its password.
  3. Confirm Replace and restore.

Everything on this phone is replaced by the backup. A wrong password or a damaged file changes nothing.

Automatic phone backup

Your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google on Android) is separate from the backup file. On Android it leaves out a big database, because Google's limit is 25 MB, so make a backup file for safety: Make a backup.

Move to a new phone

  1. On the old phone, make a backup file: Make a backup.
  2. Install Dialbook on the new phone and finish setup.
  3. Restore the file there: Restore a backup.

On Android, moving apps by cable or Wi-Fi with the phone's own transfer also brings the data across.

Delete a campaign

  1. Open the campaign.
  2. Tap the red bin at the top right and confirm Delete campaign.

This deletes its contacts, their calls, promises, payments and tasks from the phone. It cannot be undone, so make a backup first if you may need them: Make a backup.

Just want it out of the way for now? Turn off Active on the campaign's page instead: its tasks, callbacks and calls leave today's work, and nothing is deleted.

Erase everything

Go to More › Privacy and data › Erase everything on this phone. Every campaign, contact, call, task and setting is deleted from this phone. Make a backup first if you may need it.
