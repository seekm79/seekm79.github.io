Getting started

Getting started

Dialbook keeps your call lists, calls, promises and payments on this phone. There is no account and no server.

First time

  1. Choose what you use your phone calls for: Sales, Collections or both.
  2. Leave Start with sample contacts on to try the app with a few sample contacts, or turn it off to start empty.
  3. Tap Start.

Then

  • Bring in your customers from Excel or CSV: Import an Excel or CSV sheet.
  • Open a campaign and tap Start dialing to work through it: Work through a campaign.

Sales, collections or both

The app shows only the kinds of work you choose. Someone who only collects never sees a sales tab.

To change it

  1. Go to More › What I use the app for.
  2. Turn Sales or Collections on or off.

Turning one off hides its tab, reports and alerts. Its campaigns and contacts stay on the phone and come back when you turn it on again.

Add a contact by hand

  1. Open Sales or Collect.
  2. Tap + at the top, or Add lead / Add account under the campaigns.
  3. Enter at least a name and a phone number, choose the campaign, and save.

Good to know

  • A number already in that campaign is refused, so you don't call the same person twice.
  • A number marked Do not call or Invalid number in another campaign arrives already marked: The blocked-numbers list.
  • Many contacts at once? Import a sheet instead: Import an Excel or CSV sheet.

Edit a contact

  1. Open the contact.
  2. Tap the pencil (Edit) at the top right.
  3. Change the name, numbers, email, company or amounts, and save.

Changing a phone number clears its mark (Invalid number, Do not call), unless the new number is on the blocked list.

Find a contact

  1. On Home, tap the round magnifier at the top right.
  2. Type part of a name, company, email or phone number. Phone numbers match however they are written ("012-338 4471" or "0123384471").

Inside a campaign, the search box at the top of its list searches only that campaign.

Campaigns and leads

Import an Excel or CSV sheet

  1. Go to More › Import Excel or CSV (or the same row in Sales / Collect).
  2. Start from the app's own sheet: tap Download Excel template, fill it in Excel or Google Sheets, or send it to whoever has the list. The columns are already named, so matching is instant. To see how an import looks first, tap Try it with a sample sheet.
  3. Tap Choose Excel or CSV file and pick an .xlsx or .csv file. The first row must hold the column names.
  4. Match the columns: at least a name and a phone number. Company, email, amount, due date and language are optional.
  5. Choose what These contacts are for, give the list a name (List name), check the preview and import.

Dates (due date)

  • Safest: real Excel dates (cells formatted as a date), or text written as 2026-12-31, 31 Dec 2026 or Dec 31, 2026. Those can only mean one day.
  • Numbers only, such as 31/12/2026 or 12/31/2026, can be read two ways. The app checks the whole column and shows the Date format it found: Day/Month/Year or Month/Day/Year. If the sheet never shows which (every day is 12 or under), you choose one before importing.
  • Keep one style for the whole column. Dates that don't fit the chosen order are left out, and the saved list of skipped rows says why.
  • Years must be between 2000 and 2100; a two-digit year such as 26 is read as 2026.

Amounts (deal value, amount owed)

  • Plain numbers work best: 4250 or 4250.00.
  • These are also read correctly: 4,250.00, RM 4,250, MYR 1,000, $1,250, and 1.234,56 (a comma as the decimal point).
  • Avoid negatives: -500, 500- or (500) are refused, because an account cannot owe less than nothing. Avoid text such as "approx 5k" or "TBC".
  • Amounts are not converted between currencies; they are shown in the currency set in Settings.

Phone numbers

  • Any style works: 012-338 4471, 0123384471 or +60 12-338 4471 are the same number.
  • A second number can go in its own column (office line, other mobile).
  • Rows without a phone number, and repeated numbers, are left out.

Good to know

  • Big sheets (even a million rows) import in the background; a bar at the bottom shows progress and you can keep working. Cancel removes everything it added.
  • Numbers already marked Do not call or Invalid number arrive marked, and the summary says how many.
  • Each import becomes a new campaign.

Say what a campaign is about

A short line like "Office cleaning, 10% off for new customers" helps you on calls and helps the AI write messages and scripts for that campaign.

  1. Open the campaign.
  2. Tap What this campaign is about and type it.

Delete a campaign

  1. Open the campaign.
  2. Tap the red bin at the top right and confirm Delete campaign.

This deletes its contacts, their calls, promises, payments and tasks from the phone. It cannot be undone, so make a backup first if you may need them: Make a backup.

Just want it out of the way for now? Turn off Active on the campaign's page instead: its tasks, callbacks and calls leave today's work, and nothing is deleted.

Share a lead with a teammate

  1. Open the contact.
  2. Tap the QR icon (Share lead) at the top.
  3. Let your teammate scan the QR code with their phone. Include my last note adds your last call note.

Your teammate scans it in Receive a lead: Receive a lead from a teammate. Nothing goes through a server or the internet; the lead travels inside the QR code.

Receive a lead from a teammate

  1. Go to More › Receive a lead (also in Sales / Collect).
  2. The camera opens. Point it at the QR code on your teammate's phone (they open the contact and tap Share lead).
  3. Check the lead, choose Add to campaign, and tap Add lead.

If the number is already in your list, you are told before adding. The added lead gets a call back for today with your teammate's note.

Calling

Work through a campaign

  1. Open Sales or Collect and tap a campaign.
  2. Tap the green Start dialing button.
  3. Tap the big phone number: your phone's dialer opens. Talk.
  4. Come back, pick the Result of this call, add a note if needed, and tap Save and next.

The order puts callbacks due and broken promises first, then the most valuable. Skip moves on without logging. Numbers marked Do not call or Invalid number are never dialled.

Need a moment with this customer first (a task, a WhatsApp)? Tap Save instead: the call is logged and they stay on screen until you tap Next.

Log what happened on a call

After the call, pick one result under Result of this call:

  • Callback: choose the day; a call-back task is made: Call backs.
  • Promise to pay: enter the amount and the date they promised: Record a promise to pay.
  • Partial payment / Paid in full: records the payment: Record a payment.
  • Wrong number: marks that number Invalid number everywhere.
  • Your own results: Your own call results.

Add what was said in the note box, then Save and next. The result, note and time go into the contact's Call history.

Call backs

Logging Callback sets the day to call again. On that day the contact comes first in the dialer, shows in Up next on Home, and appears as a Call back task.

  • Change the day or add a time in the task: open All tasks and tap it.
  • Ticking the task off clears the call back.
  • Any other result on the next call clears it too.

Skip a contact

In the dialer, tap Skip to move to the next contact without logging anything. They stay in the queue and come up again next time.

Mark a number: OK, invalid or do not call

  1. Open the contact and tap the phone number.
  2. Choose OK, Invalid number or Do not call.

In the dialer, Mark this number under the call button does the same.

  • Marked numbers are never dialled; with a second number left, that one is used.
  • A contact with no number left to call drops out of the dialer and "left to call".
  • Do not call still lets you WhatsApp or email, with a warning first.
  • The mark applies to that number in every campaign: The blocked-numbers list.

The blocked-numbers list

Every number marked Invalid number or Do not call goes on one list for the whole app. Numbers match by their last 9 digits, so "012-338 4471" and "+60 12-338 4471" are the same number.

  • Marking a number in one campaign marks it in all of them.
  • Imports, new contacts and shared leads arrive already marked; the import summary says how many.
  • Setting it back to OK clears it everywhere.

To mark a number

  1. Open the contact and tap the phone number.
  2. Choose Do not call or Invalid number (or OK to clear it).

More: Mark a number: OK, invalid or do not call.

Call length from the phone's call log (Android)

On Android the app can read the time and length of each call from the phone's call log and attach them to the logged call. Not every version has it: the Google Play version may leave it out, and Phone call log then says so.

  1. Go to More › Phone call log.
  2. Turn on Attach call time and length automatically and allow the permission.

iPhones don't let apps read the call log, so this is Android only.

Tasks and alerts

Tasks

Tasks are things to do: Call back, Appointment, WhatsApp, Email, Research, Waiting for info or Other.

  • See them all in Tasks: Overdue, Due today and Coming up, or Done. Open it from Home › All tasks.
  • Tap the circle, or swipe the task to the right, to tick it off. Swipe it to the left to move it to Tomorrow.
  • Tap a task to open it on one page: Call now, Send WhatsApp or Send email, change the day with one tap, add Updates, and tap Mark as done. Every change saves at once.
  • Sending a WhatsApp or email to that contact ticks off a matching open task.

Add one: Add a task.

Add a task

  • From a contact: in their Tasks group, tap Add task.
  • From the dialer: tap Add task below the note.
  • On its own: in Tasks, tap +.

Choose the Kind, write what to do (or what you are waiting for), pick the day (Today, Tomorrow or Pick a day), add a time if you like, and tap Save.

Needs attention

The Needs attention box on Home shows what is late or due today: overdue tasks, tasks due today, promises to pay due today and accounts with a broken promise. Each line opens the list behind it.

The red number on Home counts what is due today or late. When nothing is, it disappears.

Sales

Pipeline stages

Sales leads move through New, Contacted, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, then Won or Lost.

  • Calls move them on their own: a first answered call makes a New lead Contacted; Interested makes it Qualified.
  • To set the stage yourself, open the contact and tap Stage.
  • See all stages in Sales › Pipeline, and how long deals stay in each in the Deal flow report: Deal flow by stage report.

Mark a deal won or lost

  • After a call, pick Won or Not interested.
  • Or open the contact, tap Stage and choose Won or Lost.

Won and lost deals are closed: they leave the dialer queue and count in the win rate and the Sales performance report: Sales performance report.

Collections

Record a promise to pay

  1. After the call, pick Promise to pay.
  2. Check Amount promised and Promised payment date, then save.

The promise shows on the contact under Promises to pay, in Collect › Promises, and on Home on the day it is due. If the day passes without payment, it becomes broken: Broken promises.

Record a payment

  • During a call: pick Partial payment (enter Amount paid) or Paid in full.
  • Any time: open the contact and tap Record payment.

The amount owed goes down, a promise it meets is marked kept, and the payment shows under Payments received and in the reports.

Broken promises

A promise is broken when its day passes without payment. Those accounts come first in the dialer, show in Needs attention on Home, and are marked on their row.

To chase them: tap the broken-promise line in Needs attention, then work down the list, or open the Collections forecast to see which promises are likely to come in: Collections forecast report.

Money owed by how late it is

Accounts are grouped by how late payment is: not yet due, 1–30 days, 31–60 days and over 60 days.

  • Home shows the totals; tap a bar to see who is in it.
  • Collect › Accounts lists accounts by how late they are.
  • The Recovery by age report shows how much of each group came in, so you can chase where it pays: Recovery by age report.

WhatsApp and email

Send a WhatsApp from a template

  1. Open the contact (or use WhatsApp in the dialer).
  2. Tap WhatsApp, pick a template, check the text and send.

WhatsApp opens with the message typed in; you press send there. The send is recorded on the contact under WhatsApp and email sent.

Make or change templates: Make or change message templates.

Send an email from a template

  1. Open the contact and tap Email.
  2. Pick a template, check the subject and text, and choose your email app.

Your email app opens with it filled in; you press send there. The send is recorded under WhatsApp and email sent.

Make or change message templates

  1. Go to More › Message templates.
  2. Choose WhatsApp or Email, then tap + for a new one, or a template to change it.
  3. Give it a name and the text. Tap the name tags to insert placeholders: Placeholders in messages.
  • Create with AI writes a draft from a short description (with an AI provider set up).
  • Make a version in another language makes a copy in another language; customers with that language set get it first.
  • In a WhatsApp message, select text and tap Bold / Italic / Strike for bold, italic or strikethrough. The preview shows how it looks.

Placeholders in messages

Placeholders are filled in for each contact when you send:

  • Name: the contact's name
  • Company: their company
  • Amount: deal value or amount owed
  • Due date: the due date
  • My name / My company: you and your company (set under AI assistant › About you)

The editor warns about a placeholder it doesn't know.

Let AI write a template

  1. In Message templates, tap Create with AI.
  2. Describe it under What is it for? (e.g. "polite reminder, invoice 30 days late"), choose the language, and tap Write draft.
  3. Check the draft and save it.

Only your description and what you sell go to your AI provider, never customer details. Needs an AI provider: Set up the AI assistant.

Sales performance report

Part of Dialbook Pro. The sales scoreboard: how much selling got done, and what it produced, over a period.

What it is for

To see whether the effort (calls, people reached) is turning into results (deals won, value won), and whether this period is better or worse than the one before.

What it shows

  • Figures at the top: value won, deals won, win rate, new leads, and the change against the period before.
  • A chart of value won per day, week or month.
  • A table per period with calls, reached, won, value won, lost, win rate and new leads.

How to use it

  • Group by week to see the trend; a week with many calls but no wins points at the pitch, a week with few calls points at the effort.
  • Win rate is deals won out of deals closed (won plus lost). A falling rate with steady calls means leads are being lost later in the pipeline: look at Deal flow by stage report.
  • New leads tells you whether the top of the funnel is filling. If it isn't, import or add contacts before the pipeline runs dry.

Choose the Period at the top: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time (the longer periods are part of Dialbook Pro). Use Group by to add the figures up per Day, Week or Month. Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Sales performance.

Payments received (by period) report

Part of Dialbook Pro. The collections scoreboard: money that came in, and promises made, over a period.

What it is for

To see how much was collected, from how many customers, and whether promises are being made at the pace needed, against the period before.

What it shows

  • Figures at the top: collected, number of payments, average a day, promises made, and the change against the period before.
  • A chart of money collected per day, week or month.
  • A table per period with payments, collected, customers paying, promises made and the amount promised.

How to use it

  • Group by week: a week where promised is high but collected is low is a week of promises that will need chasing. See Promises to pay report.
  • Average a day, times the working days left, gives a quick forecast of the month; compare with Target pace report.
  • Many payments from few customers means the same people keep paying: the rest of the list needs calling.

Choose the Period at the top: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time (the longer periods are part of Dialbook Pro). Use Group by to add the figures up per Day, Week or Month. Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Payments received.

Calls, WhatsApp and email report

Part of Dialbook Pro. Every way you touched customers, and how many you actually reached.

What it is for

To measure activity honestly: calls alone understate the work when half the follow-up goes by WhatsApp or email, and calls that nobody answered are not contact.

What it shows

  • Figures at the top: calls, people reached, WhatsApps, emails, and the change against the period before.
  • A chart of calls, WhatsApps and emails per day, week or month.
  • A table per period with calls, reached, reach rate, WhatsApp, email and the total.

How to use it

  • Reach rate is the share of calls that got a person. Below about half, try other hours: Best time to call report.
  • If messages go up while calls go down, check that messages are followed by results: What works best report.
  • Use it for a daily or weekly activity target when a team shares one list.

Choose the Period at the top: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time (the longer periods are part of Dialbook Pro). Use Group by to add the figures up per Day, Week or Month. Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Calls, WhatsApp and email.

Target pace report

Part of Dialbook Pro. How far you are from this month's target and what each remaining day must bring.

What it is for

To turn a monthly target into a daily number, early enough to change something. It needs a target: Monthly targets.

What it shows

  • Days left this month.
  • A row per work type (sales, collections) with the target, so far, the share reached, needed a day, and where this pace ends the month ("on pace for").
  • A chart of the target against what has come in so far.

How to use it

  • "Needed a day" is the figure to carry into each morning. If it keeps rising, the month is slipping.
  • "On pace for" below the target with a week to go means today's work must change, not next week's.
  • Set the target for both sales value won and money collected if you do both.

It always covers the current month. Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Target pace.

Collections forecast report

Part of Dialbook Pro. The promised payments still to come, weighted by each customer's record of keeping promises.

What it is for

To know how much cash is really likely in the next weeks, rather than the total of everything promised. A customer who has broken three promises counts for less than one who has kept every one.

What it shows

  • Figures at the top: promised in the next 7 days, promised in the next 30 days, and the amount likely to come in.
  • A chart of promised against likely, by week.
  • A table per pending promise: due date, contact, promised amount, how often that customer keeps promises, and the likely amount.

How to use it

  • Chase the large promises with a low "keeps promises" rate first; they move the likely figure most.
  • A customer with no history uses the overall rate of everyone's promises.
  • Compare the likely figure with the target in Target pace report; the gap is what new promises must cover.

It looks ahead from today; the period does not apply. Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Collections forecast.

Recovery by age report

Part of Dialbook Pro. Of the money owed at the start of the period, how much came in, grouped by how late it was.

What it is for

To see where chasing pays. Accounts under 30 days late usually recover well; the rate over 60 days tells you whether old debt is worth the calls or should be handled differently.

What it shows

  • Figures at the top: recovered overall and the amount collected.
  • A chart of recovery rate by lateness: not yet due, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, over 60 days.
  • A table with accounts, owed at the start of the period, collected during it, and the share recovered, per group.

How to use it

  • If the 31 to 60 group recovers far better than over 60, the lesson is to call sooner: the window is the second month.
  • A low rate in "not yet due" is normal; a low rate in 1 to 30 days is not, and points at reminders not going out. See Send a WhatsApp from a template.
  • Use Last month for a clean month-to-month comparison.

Choose the Period at the top: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time (the longer periods are part of Dialbook Pro). Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Recovery by age.

Deal flow by stage report

Part of Dialbook Pro. How deals move through the pipeline: entering each stage, how long they stay, and how many are stuck.

What it is for

To find the stage where deals slow down or die. A pipeline total hides this; the flow shows it.

What it shows

  • Figures at the top: deals won and deals lost in the period.
  • A chart of deals entering each stage.
  • A table per stage: open now, entered in the period, moved on, average days in the stage, and how many have been stuck 14 days or more.

How to use it

  • A stage with many entering and few moving on is the bottleneck. Proposal is the usual one: quotations sent and never followed up.
  • "Stuck 14+ days" is a call list. Open Sales › Pipeline, tap that stage, and work through it.
  • Average days in stage, added up, is your real sales cycle. Use it when forecasting.

Choose the Period at the top: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time (the longer periods are part of Dialbook Pro). Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Deal flow by stage.

What works best report

Part of Dialbook Pro. Which channel, and which message template, is followed by a result within 7 days.

What it is for

To stop guessing which message to send. A result is a payment, a promise to pay, a step forward in the pipeline, or a call with a good outcome in the 7 days after the message.

What it shows

  • Figures at the top: calls, WhatsApps and emails in the period.
  • A chart of the result rate per channel.
  • A table of every template that was sent: channel, template, times sent, results within 7 days, and the rate. Messages typed by hand are shown as one line.

How to use it

  • Templates sent fewer than 5 times are too few to judge; send more before deciding.
  • If a reminder has a low rate, change its wording or add the invoice as a PDF: Make or change message templates.
  • Compare WhatsApp with email for the same purpose, and lead with the better one.

Choose the Period at the top: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time (the longer periods are part of Dialbook Pro). Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › What works best.

Daily activity report

Included in the free version. Every call in the period, one line each, with its result and note.

What it is for

The log. Use it to check a day's work, to hand over a list to a colleague, or to answer "what did we say to this customer last week".

What it shows

  • Figures at the top: calls, people reached, and how many different contacts.
  • A table with date, time, contact, campaign and result, with the note under it.

How to use it

  • Choose today for an end-of-day check, or Last month to export a month's log as CSV.
  • Long periods are cut to the first rows on screen; share as CSV to get every row.

Choose the Period at the top: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time (the longer periods are part of Dialbook Pro). Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Daily activity.

Call results report

Included in the free version. How many calls ended in each result.

What it is for

To see the shape of the work: how much of it is no answer, how much is callbacks, and how much ends in a promise, a payment or a sale.

What it shows

  • Figures at the top: calls and people reached.
  • A chart of calls per result.
  • A table with each result, its count and its share of all calls.

How to use it

  • A large "No answer" share says when you call matters more than what you say: Best time to call report.
  • Many callbacks and few promises means the second call is not closing. Prepare the second call differently, or ask the AI for a strategy on the contact.
  • Your own results count too, under the name you gave them: Your own call results.

Choose the Period at the top: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time (the longer periods are part of Dialbook Pro). Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Call results.

Campaign performance report

Included in the free version. Every campaign side by side: contacts, called, reached, converted, and what is left today.

What it is for

To compare lists. A trade-show list and a renewals list behave differently; this shows which deserves the next hour.

What it shows

  • A chart of conversion per campaign.
  • A table per campaign: contacts, called, reached, won or paid, conversion, and left to call today.

How to use it

  • Conversion is won or paid out of contacts called. Low conversion with high reach means the offer or the list is wrong, not the calling.
  • "Left today" is the queue; a campaign with a big queue and good conversion is where the day should go.
  • Campaigns that are finished can be turned off so they leave today's work: Delete a campaign.

Choose the Period at the top: today, 7 or 30 days, This month, Last month or All time (the longer periods are part of Dialbook Pro). Share or print it with the button at the top right: Share or print a report.

Open it: Reports › Campaign performance.

Best time to call report

Part of Dialbook Pro. Reach rate by hour of the day.

What it is for

To find the hours when people pick up, so calls go where they land.

What it shows

  • The best hour at the top.

AI assistant

Set up the AI assistant

  1. Go to More › AI assistant.
  2. Choose a Provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek and others), enter the API key, stored on this phone only and choose a model.
  3. Read the data notice and tap Turn on.

The key is stored on this phone only. Nothing is sent until you turn it on. Privacy: What the AI sees.

What the AI sees

The assistant looks up what it needs on this phone; only those answers go to your AI provider, never your whole database.

Turn on Remove names and phone numbers before sending (in AI assistant) and names, companies, emails and phone numbers are taken out before anything is sent. Contacts are then known to the AI by number only, and the app shows the real names in the chat.

AI chats and history

  • New chat (the pencil at the top right) starts a new chat; the current one is kept.
  • Delete chat (the red bin at the top) deletes the chat on screen, after asking.
  • History (the clock) lists earlier chats: tap one to read or continue it, swipe left to delete it.
  • Each question sends only the latest part of a chat, and a chat holds 20 questions, to keep each question cheap. For a new topic, start a new chat.
  • "Looked up: …" above an answer says which of your data it used. An answer marked "not checked" gave figures without looking them up: ask again.

Show or hide the AI

Go to More › AI assistant and turn Show AI in the app on or off. Off hides Ask AI about your work on Home and the AI buttons on contacts; your settings and chats are kept.

Backup and data

Make a backup

  1. Go to More › Backup and restore.
  2. Set a Backup password (keep it safe: without it the backup cannot be opened).
  3. Tap Create backup and share and save the file to Google Drive, email or your computer.

The file is encrypted. Remind me to back up sets how often Home reminds you.

Restore a backup

  1. Go to More › Backup and restore.
  2. Tap Restore from a backup file, pick the file and enter its password.
  3. Confirm Replace and restore.

Everything on this phone is replaced by the backup. A wrong password or a damaged file changes nothing.

Automatic phone backup

Your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google on Android) is separate from the backup file. On Android, Google's backup leaves out Dialbook's data (its limit is 25 MB, far less than a big contact list), so make a backup file for safety: Make a backup. Your backup password and AI key never move to another phone this way.

Move to a new phone

  1. On the old phone, make a backup file: Make a backup.
  2. Install Dialbook on the new phone and finish setup.
  3. Restore the file there: Restore a backup.

On Android, moving apps by cable or Wi-Fi with the phone's own transfer also brings the data across.

Your backup password and AI key stay on the old phone: enter them again on the new one.

Erase everything

Go to More › Privacy and data › Erase everything on this phone. Every campaign, contact, call, task and setting is deleted from this phone, with your backup password, your AI key and the files the app made for sharing. Only the day your free trial started is kept. Make a backup first if you may need it.
