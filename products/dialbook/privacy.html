1. Who we are

Dialbook is made by SKM Studio ("we", "us"). We are the developer of the app and the publisher of this policy. You can reach us at kuanmeng.see@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores, and where

Dialbook is a call-list CRM. To do its job it stores the information you put into it:

  • Contacts: names, phone numbers, companies, email addresses, preferred language and notes.
  • Your work on them: calls and their results, pipeline stages, tasks, promises to pay, payments, amounts owed and due dates.
  • Campaigns, message templates with their pictures and files, call-result names and monthly targets.
  • Settings, including your name, company and what you sell for message fill-ins, the currency, the language and appearance.
  • If you set them: your backup password and your AI provider's API key, stored in the operating system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, encrypted storage on Android).
  • Your AI chats, if you use the assistant.

All of this is stored in a database on your device only. Dialbook has no server and no user accounts. We do not receive, store, or have any way to read your data. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software.

4. The AI assistant

The AI assistant is off by default. To use it you connect your own account with an AI provider, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, or a model running on your own computer, and enter that provider's API key. We provide no AI service and see none of your requests, your key, or the replies.

When the assistant is on, each question you ask, and each strategy or message draft you request, sends the details it needs to that provider over the internet. The assistant looks up what it needs on your phone and sends only those answers, never your whole database. The provider handles what it receives under its own privacy policy and data-retention settings, which you should read before you start.

Masking. With masking on, names, companies, email addresses and phone numbers are removed before anything is sent. Contacts are then known to the AI by number only, and the app shows the real names in the chat. Amounts, dates and your notes are still sent.

Before the assistant can be turned on, the app shows a data notice and asks you to accept responsibility for the data you send. It records the date you accepted. You can turn the assistant off, hide it, or delete your chats at any time in Settings.

You are responsible for making sure you are allowed to share your customers' personal and financial details with your AI provider under the data protection laws that apply to you.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan a teammate's lead QR code. Asked when you first open Receive a lead. No photos are taken or stored.
  • Photos and files: only when you attach a picture or PDF to a message template, or pick a spreadsheet or backup file to import. The file you choose is copied into the app; nothing else is read.
  • Internet: only for the AI assistant, the day map, and purchases. Everything else works offline.
  • Phone call log (Android only, optional): if you turn on "attach call length" in Settings, the app reads the duration of calls you made from the app and attaches it to the logged call. It is off by default, asks for permission only when you turn it on, and the data never leaves the phone. iPhones do not allow apps to read the call log.
  • Phone calls: Dialbook does not make calls itself. It opens your phone's dialer with the number filled in and you press call.

6. Purchases

Dialbook Pro is bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their terms and privacy policies; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase with the store to unlock Pro features. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

7. Security

  • Backup files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your password. Nobody can open a backup without the password, including us. If you lose the password, the backup cannot be recovered.
  • Your backup password and AI key are kept in the operating system's secure storage.
  • Requests to your AI provider use HTTPS.
  • Your device's passcode, biometrics and full-disk encryption protect the database, as they do for any app. Keep your device locked.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it. You can delete a single contact or campaign, erase everything in Settings › Privacy and data, or uninstall the app, which removes the database. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backup files you saved elsewhere, and data held by your AI provider, are under your and their control.

9. Your customers' data

The people in your call lists are your customers, prospects or debtors. Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia), the GDPR, or the law in your market, you are the one responsible for that data: for having a lawful reason to hold it, for honouring do-not-call requests, and for deciding whether it may be sent to an AI provider. Dialbook gives you tools for this, such as the do-not-call mark that stops a number being dialled in every campaign, but the decisions are yours.

10. Children

Dialbook is a work tool and is not directed at children under 13, or the age of digital consent in your country. We do not knowingly collect any information from children; we do not collect information from anyone.
