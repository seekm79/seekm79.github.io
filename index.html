Dialbook

iPhone · iPad · Android
Call-list CRM for sales follow-up and debt collection

Dialbook is for anyone who works from a list of people to call. Import a spreadsheet, work through it from the dialer, log what happened, and never miss a callback or a promise to pay. Everything stays on the phone: there is no account and no server.

  • ✓ Sales pipeline and collections in one app
  • ✓ Promises to pay and a broken-promise queue
  • ✓ Imports Excel or CSV, even a million rows
  • ✓ WhatsApp, SMS and email templates
  • ✓ Encrypted backups you control
  • ✓ Bring your own AI, with masking
